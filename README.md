# Project: The Louisiana Benefits Cliff Tracker

### 1. Purpose and Authority
This application is an independent tool designed to assist Louisiana residents in navigating the "Benefits Cliff." It provides a data-driven projection of how changes in household income affect eligibility for state-administered programs, including SNAP and Medicaid. 

### 2. Data Sources & Logic
The calculations within this tool are based on the eligibility thresholds provided by:
* **Louisiana Department of Children & Family Services (DCFS):** SNAP Income Limits (2024-2025).
* **Louisiana Department of Health (LDH):** Medicaid/LaCHIP Eligibility Standards.
* **Bureau of Labor Statistics:** Cost-of-living adjustments for the Louisiana region.

### 3. AI-Augmented Development (Technical Disclosure)
In alignment with modern engineering standards, this project utilized AI-assisted development to architect the initial logic engine. 

### 4. Disclaimer
*This tool is for informational purposes only and is not an official determination of benefits. Residents should always verify their status through [LA Department of Health](https://ldh.la.gov/).*
